Interactive DSLR Photography Companion App Concept
Project Overview
The goal is to build a slick, modern Progressive Web App (PWA) that acts as an interactive companion for DSLR users, specifically tailored to the user's specific camera body and lens inventory. The app will feel like a native mobile app, utilizing the phone's hardware to analyze lighting conditions and recommend optimal camera settings, while also serving as an interactive, visual user manual.
Hardware Profile
The app will customize its tips, guides, and settings recommendations based on the user's specific gear:
Camera Body
	•	Nikon D7100 (referenced as 7D7100)
Current Lenses
	•	AF-S Nikkor 55-200mm f/4-5.6G ED (referenced as f/1.4-5.6G)
	•	AF-S Nikkor 35mm f/1.8G
	•	Upcoming Lens: A mid-range lens (focal length sitting between 35mm and 55mm) currently in transit.
Key Features & Functionality
1. Interactive 3D Camera Guide
	•	Photorealistic 3D Render: A bold, modern, high-fidelity 3D render of the Nikon D7100 and attached lenses.
	•	Interactive Hotspots: Users can tap on various buttons and dials on the 3D model to see what they do and how to use them.
	•	"How-To" Settings Tab: Step-by-step instructions showing exactly how to change specific settings on the physical camera body (e.g., how to adjust ISO, white balance, or exposure compensation).
2. Environment & Lighting Analyzer
	•	Mobile Camera Integration: Use the smartphone's camera to snap a photo of the current environment or lighting challenge.
	•	Smart Recommendations: The app analyzes the lighting conditions and suggests specific aperture, shutter speed, and ISO settings based on the currently attached lens.
3. Smart Troubleshooting & Beginner Guides
	•	Common Lighting Challenges: Interactive guides for tricky scenarios, such as:
	•	High-contrast environments
	•	Overcast/pure white skies
	•	Split-lighting scenes (where one half of the frame is dark and the other is bright)
	•	Interactive Manual Backing: Import and parse the official Nikon D7100 operating manual to act as the primary data source and "backing content" for troubleshooting queries.
4. Educational Resources
	•	Quick-reference notes on the "Exposure Triangle" (Aperture, Shutter Speed, ISO).
	•	Guides on lens mechanics, lens covers, and popular attachments (filters, hoods, etc.).
UI/UX Design Direction
	•	Aesthetic: Slick, bold, and highly modern.
	•	Feel: Highly responsive PWA that mimics the smooth transitions and tactile feel of a native iOS/Android mobile application.
	•	Visual Assets: High-quality, photorealistic renders of the Nikon body and lens ecosystem.
